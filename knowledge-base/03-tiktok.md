## What this means

TikTok aggressively blocks non-browser clients; the profile, bio, follower/like counts, and
video list are **not retrievable by automated fetch**. As with Instagram, an agent would hit
the same wall.

## How to enrich

A **Claude-in-Chrome** pass on a logged-in session can capture the bio, follower/like counts,
pinned/recent video topics, captions, sounds, and posting cadence — useful for understanding
brand voice and content pillars. The brand could also share this directly.
