01 — Colour

Warm neutrals, ink, one gold.

Sampled from the logo. Use neutrals for ~85% of any surface, ink for text, and brass as a single sparing accent. Restraint is the brand.

Suggested surface ratio — washi / ink / brass.

05 — Voice

Few words. Lots of space.

A quiet table by the lake. Sushi at the bar. Time, slowed.
We're resting. Back to the bar soon.

Do

  • Lead with space — when unsure, add ma, not elements.
  • Keep gold for one accent per view.
  • Embrace asymmetry & hand-made texture.
  • Write short, sensory, calm.

Don't

  • No gloss, hard shadows, or neon.
  • No exclamation spam or emoji clutter.
  • No rigid symmetry or stock-photo sheen.
  • Don't recolour or distort the logo.
